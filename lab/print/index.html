Print a scene

Every asset of the episode, checked for a standard FDM printer (0.4 mm nozzle) at board-game scale: a figure about 6 cm, a castle about 15 cm. Tick what you want, change a size if you like, and download the STL files. One three.js part is one printed piece, in its own colour.

AssetSize (mm)PiecesBefore printing

In the zip, per asset: <id>.stl (every piece in one file, for a one-colour print), pieces/ (one STL per piece, named with its colour, for painting or colour changes), and PRINT.txt (sizes and what to fix). « open » is a piece whose surface is not closed, a slicer may refuse or misread it; « flat » has no thickness (a flag, a door), it needs one; « thin » is under 1 mm, two nozzle lines or fewer; « floating » touches nothing (a gem above a head), it needs a pin or a stand. The export fixes them (lib/print.js repair, each fix listed in PRINT.txt): open pieces capped, sheets thickened to 1 mm, thin ones widened to 1 mm, floating ones pinned to the nearest piece above or below, and a landmark cut at its ground so it stands flat.

Grid: 1 cm squares on the bed. Red: a piece to fix before printing. Amber: fixed for printing (hide). Drag to turn.
