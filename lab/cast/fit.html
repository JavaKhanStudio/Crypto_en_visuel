The cast: does it go together, and does it print? (lib/fit.js)

Each figure is checked at the size it prints, from what the export would print, in voxels of 0.2 mm, and drawn ghosted with what is wrong: two parts in the same space a ring, the head or the hat stopped on its way down the neck pin a bit that prints loose from its part a wall under 1 mm not a fault: the slot cut in the robe for the arms to swing in (up to 35° forward). Sockets are cut by what goes in them (the neck pin, a hat's peg, an arm's shoulder pin, the head under a hat), with 0.2 mm of play; the hat's peg and each arm's pin end in a bulb 0.5 mm wider than the socket's lip, so they snap in. An arm, with what it holds, is swung through its whole range and must meet nothing (red if it does).
