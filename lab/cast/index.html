The cast as toys (lib/cast.js), round 2

Every figure is five pieces that come apart. Body: robe, arms and hands in one print, the kind's colour, a socket at the neck, a hole through the right fist. Rings: every ring drops over the neck pin and sits on the shoulders; what hangs from it (a scarf tail, a toga's sash, a strap, a tabard) hangs between the arms, so it goes on and off with the arms printed on. The head holds it down. Head: the face and its fixed trait, a pin under it (the neck, hidden once assembled), a stud on top. Hat: hair or a hat pressed onto the stud. Eve's is always black. Item: pinned into the fist. Printed 40 mm tall: neck pin 3.5 mm, fist hole 2 mm, rings 1.1 mm thick.

What never changes (the kind): Alice: blue robe, bun, blue scarf ring. Bob: yellow robe, leather belt with a gold buckle, flush with the robe. Eve: red robe, black mask with white eyes, a black hat. Messenger: the satchel (strap, wax seal, post horn), the piece the others can borrow.
